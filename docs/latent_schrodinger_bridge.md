# 32x32 latent Schrödinger bridge

## Endpoint measures

Let (E_\theta:\mathcal X\to[-1,1]^{8\times32\times32}) and
(G_\theta) be the learned AFHQ autoencoder. The empirical latent endpoint
measures are

\[
\mu_0^N=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N\delta_{E_\theta(x_i^{cat})},\qquad
\mu_1^M=\frac1M\sum_{j=1}^M\delta_{E_\theta(x_j^{dog})}.
\]

The 32x32 latent retains spatial identity that was lost in the earlier 8x8
beta-VAE. On held-out AFHQ, image L1 falls from 0.0586 to 0.0280 and edge
energy rises from approximately 0.0126 to 0.0224 (real images: 0.0367).

## DINO content coupling

DINO supplies content descriptors with the empirical species direction
projected out. The finite endpoint plan solves

\[
\pi_\varepsilon=\arg\min_{\pi\in\Pi(a,b)}
\langle C_{DINO},\pi\rangle+
\varepsilon\operatorname{KL}(\pi\Vert a\otimes b).
\]

This uses DINO only to decide which distinct cat and dog identities are
compatible. Image formation and latent dynamics receive no DINO gradient.
The 256-by-256 training plan reached marginal errors below (3\times10^{-11}).

## Reciprocal Brownian mixture

Conditioned on a sampled endpoint pair ((Z_0,Z_1)\sim\pi_\varepsilon), the
latent reference bridge is

\[
Z_t=(1-t)Z_0+tZ_1+\sqrt{2\tau}\,(W_t-tW_1).
\]

Consequently,

\[
Z_t\mid Z_0,Z_1\sim
\mathcal N\left((1-t)Z_0+tZ_1,\;2\tau t(1-t)I\right).
\]

The implementation uses six displayed macro intervals and four numerical
microsteps per interval. The accepted stochastic example uses
(\tau=5\times10^{-4}). Its noise is temporally correlated and exactly zero
at both endpoints.

The decoded observation path is (X_t=G_\theta(Z_t)). Real endpoint images
are pinned after decoding, while every interior frame is generated by the
same decoder.

## Learned marginal-field ablation

A 526k-parameter convolutional field was also trained on the reciprocal
mixture. For sampled bridges it regressed

\[
v^*(Z_t,t)=Z_1-Z_0+
\frac{\tau(1-2t)}{\sqrt{2\tau t(1-t)}}\,\xi,
\qquad
\sqrt{2\tau t(1-t)}s^*(Z_t,t)=-\xi.
\]

Two regularizers enforce time reversal and spatially smooth drift. Although
flow MSE fell from 0.298 to approximately 0.10 and reciprocal error from 0.46
to 0.009, its integrated marginal flow remained close to the identity on five
unseen cats. The score head also stayed at its unit-noise baseline. This
finite-pool marginal field is rejected.

The exact endpoint-conditioned components are accepted. They visibly move all
five distinct cats into five distinct dogs, retain substantially more semantic
detail than the 8x8 VAE, and do not exhibit the colored pixel artifacts of the
post-hoc residual diffusion decoder.

## Limitation

The coupling cost is DINO content cost, whereas the conditional reference
diffusion is Euclidean in the learned latent. Therefore this is an empirical
reciprocal bridge with a semantically chosen endpoint plan, not the exact
Schrödinger minimizer for a single common latent Brownian reference cost. A
fully consistent next version would learn a latent metric (g_\eta) and use
its induced heat kernel in both Sinkhorn and the bridge dynamics.
